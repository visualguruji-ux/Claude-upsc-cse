## Part 3 — Exam value-addition (from NCERT & standard sources)

### Erosion vs Weathering (classic trap)

| Aspect | Weathering | Erosion |
|---|---|---|
| Nature | **Static** (no movement) | **Dynamic** (movement) |
| Location | **In-situ** (in place) | **Ex-situ** (carried away) |
| Transporting agent | Not required | **Required** |
| Result | Rock breaks down → regolith | Landscape lowered & sculpted |

**One-line link:** Weathering *prepares* the loose material (regolith); erosion *removes and
transports* it. Both, with mass movement, are **degradational (destructional)** geomorphic processes.

### Agents of erosion & their zones
- **Running water (rivers)** — most important agent; humid regions → valleys, gorges, waterfalls, badlands.
- **Glaciers (moving ice)** — high latitudes/mountains → U-shaped valleys, cirques, horns, arêtes.
- **Wind (aeolian)** — hot deserts/arid lands → mushroom rocks, yardangs, deflation hollows.
- **Sea waves** — coasts → sea cliffs, caves, arches, stacks, wave-cut platforms.
- **Groundwater** — limestone/karst → sinkholes, caves, caverns (solution).

### Processes (how an agent erodes)
- **Hydraulic action** — force of moving water/waves dislodging rock.
- **Abrasion / Corrasion** — carried load grinds bed & banks.
- **Attrition** — transported fragments collide and wear each other down.
- **Corrosion / Solution** — chemical dissolving of soluble rock (limestone).
- **Deflation** — wind lifts & removes loose fine particles.

### Indian context (answer fodder)
- **Gully & ravine erosion** — Chambal ravines (MP/Rajasthan/UP badlands).
- **Sheet & rill erosion** — topsoil loss on deforested slopes → land degradation.
- **Coastal erosion** — retreating shorelines (Kerala, Odisha, Tamil Nadu).
- **Wind erosion** — Thar desert (Rajasthan).
- **Data edge:** link topsoil loss to soil health, food security & desertification (UNCCD).
