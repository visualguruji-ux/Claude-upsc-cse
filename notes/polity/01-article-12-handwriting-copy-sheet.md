## PAGE 1

```
ART. 12 — "STATE"        (Part III, Arts 12-35)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════
"In this Part, unless the context otherwise
 requires, 'the State' INCLUDES —
   (a) Govt + Parliament of India
   (b) Govt + Legislature of each State
   (c) all LOCAL or OTHER authorities
   — within territory of India
          OR
     under control of Govt of India"

3 KEY WORDS  ⚠
 1. "includes"     → INCLUSIVE, not exhaustive
 2. "unless context otherwise requires"
                   → flexible
 3. "OR"           → DISJUNCTIVE
     ∴ body OUTSIDE India but under GoI
       control = STILL STATE

WHY IT MATTERS
  FR available against STATE only
  Art 12 = GATEKEEPER of Part III
  Art 36 → same meaning in Part IV (DPSP)
  Art 32 → writ only vs "State"

AMBEDKAR  (CAD, 25 Nov 1948)
 "every authority which has got either the
  power to make laws or the power to have
  discretion vested in it"
      ↓
 seed of INSTRUMENTALITY doctrine
```

LIMB 3 :  LOCAL AUTHORITY

CASE → UOI vs R.C. JAIN (1981) S.C.
       [DDA case]  — 5 PARAMETERS

  ┌──── L A A F S ────┐

  L — Separate Legal existence
        (body corporate, not a mere
         govt agency/dept)
  A — Functioning in a defined Area
        + "ORDINARILY elected by
           the inhabitants"        ⚠
  A — Degree of Autonomy
        (appreciable, need not be full)
  F — Power to raise Funds / revenue
        (tax, rate, charge, fee)
        ⭐ control of fund must VEST in it
  S — Entrusted by Statute with functions
        ordinarily assoc. with local govt
        → health, education, water,
          sewerage, town planning, roads,
          markets, transport, welfare

⚠ "ORDINARILY" → election NOT compulsory
   ∴ DDA (non-elected) STILL = local authority

Egs: Municipality · Panchayat · District Board
     Improvement Trust · Port Commissioners
     Town / Notified Area Cttee · DDA
