## PART A — QUIT INDIA MOVEMENT (1942)

### 1. Immediate background

| Trigger | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Cripps Mission (Mar 1942)** ⚠️ | One-man mission of **Sir Stafford Cripps**, sent under US/China pressure. Offered **Dominion Status *after* the war**, an Indian **Constituent Assembly**, and the **right of any province to opt out** (secede from Commonwealth). Defence to stay with the British. |
| **Gandhi's verdict** 🎯 | *"A post-dated cheque on a crashing/failing bank"*; *"Nothing new, nothing immediate."* |
| **Others** | Jinnah: reduced Pakistan to a "remote possibility"; **Ambedkar** disillusioned (felt it went back to the August Offer). |
| **War at the door** | Fall of **Rangoon & Singapore** → fear of **Japanese invasion** of the east (Assam, Bengal). |
| **Economic distress** | Wartime price-rise, shortages, forced recruitment, the "**denial policy**" (boat/rice seizure in Bengal-Orissa). |
| **Gandhi's radicalisation** | "Militant Gandhi" — *"Leave India to God, or to anarchy… I will address the people directly."* |

### 2. The launch

- **14 July 1942 — Wardha Resolution:** Congress Working Committee (at **Wardha**) demands **immediate British withdrawal**.
- **8 August 1942 — Quit India Resolution** ⚠️: passed by the **AICC** at **Gowalia Tank Maidan, Bombay** (later *August Kranti Maidan*). **Moved by Nehru, seconded by Patel.** (Rajagopalachari dissented.)
- 🎯 **"Do or Die" (Karo ya Maro):** *"We shall either free India or die in the attempt."* Gandhi: *"you can choose your own path"* — did **not** bind the movement to his personal non-violence.
- **9 August 1942 — arrests** (Operation "Zero Hour"): entire leadership jailed.
  - **Gandhi, Kasturba, Mahadev Desai** (+ Sarojini Naidu, Mira Behn) → **Aga Khan Palace, Poona**.
  - **CWC (Nehru, Patel, Azad…)** → **Ahmednagar Fort**.
- **Aruna Asaf Ali** hoisted the flag at Gowalia Tank on **9 Aug 1942** → "heroine of 1942"; then went underground.

### 3. Three phases 🧠 *"Urban → Rural → Underground"*

| Phase | Time | Character |
|---|---|---|
| **1st** | Aug 1942 | **Urban** — hartals, strikes, student walkouts. Crushed in weeks. |
| **2nd** | Sept–Oct 1942 | **Rural**, most **violent** — attacks on railways, telegraph, police stations, govt buildings. |
| **3rd** | Late 1942–44 | **Underground** — parallel governments, sabotage, guerrilla pockets. |

### 4. Underground network ⚠️

| Person | Contribution |
|---|---|
| **Usha Mehta** (+ Nanka Motwani) | Secret **Congress Radio**, Bombay → **4 yrs jail** when traced. |
| **Ram Manohar Lohia** | Edited underground magazine ***Inquilab*** (with Aruna Asaf Ali). |
| **Aruna Asaf Ali** | Provided funds/arms; underground leader. |
| **Biju Patnaik** | Flew **clandestine air sorties** (carried Lohia, Sucheta Kripalani). |
| **Jayaprakash Narayan** | **Azad Dasta** (Freedom Brigade), Bihar-Nepal border; escaped **Hazaribagh jail** (over the wall, Diwali 1942). |
| Others | Sucheta Kripalani, Achyut Patwardhan, Chhotubhai Puranik; Ramnath Goenka & K.G. Mashruwala (subversive press). |

### 5. Parallel governments (prati sarkars) ⚠️⚠️

| Place | Leader | Name / features |
|---|---|---|
| **Ballia** (E-UP) | **Chittu Pandey** | Active only ~a week; freed prisoners, repealed repressive laws. |
| **Tamluk** (Midnapore, WB) | **Satish Chandra Samanta** | ***Tamralipta Jatiya Sarkar***; arm = **Vidyut Vahini**; 1942–44. |
| **Satara** (Maharashtra) | **Nana Patil** (with Y.B. Chavan) | ***Prati Sarkar*** — **longest-lived** (to 1945–46); arm = **Toofan Sena**; *nyaydan mandals* (courts), "Gandhi marriages"; wound up on Gandhi's request. |
| Others | — | Swaraj Panchayat at **Contai**; **Talcher** (Orissa); pockets in Bihar, Assam, Andhra. |

🧠 **Ballia–Tamluk–Satara = "Big 3 Prati Sarkars."**

### 6. Martyrs

- **Matangini Hazra** — 73-yr "**Gandhi Buri**", Tamluk; shot holding the flag aloft (Vidyut Vahini).
- **Kanaklata Barua** — teenaged girl, **Gohpur, Assam** (**Mrityu Bahini**); shot hoisting the flag.
- **Laxman Nayak** — tribal Gandhian, **Koraput/Talcher (Orissa)**.
- Casualties: official ~**1,100 killed**, ~**1 lakh jailed** (nationalist estimates higher).

### 7. Who stayed away ⚠️

| Group | Stand |
|---|---|
| **Communists (CPI)** | "**People's War**" line (support the anti-fascist Allies after USSR was attacked). |
| **Muslim League** | Movement seen as coercing the British to hand power to "Hindu Congress." |
| **Hindu Mahasabha** | Savarkar: *"Stick to your posts."* |
| **Princely states** | Muted response. |
| **B.R. Ambedkar** | Called a mass movement then "irresponsible"; joined the Viceroy's Council (Labour member). |
| **Higher bureaucracy** | Remained loyal (lower ranks sometimes sympathetic). |

### 8. Suppression & significance

- **Lord Linlithgow:** *"the most serious rebellion since 1857."* Tools: emergency powers, **Defence of India Rules**, Criminal Law Amendment Act, **collective fines, whipping, aerial/​machine-gun firing**.
- **Feb 1943 — Gandhi's 21-day fast** (answer to the charge that he caused the violence). Released **6 May 1944** (health). **Mahadev Desai (1942)** and **Kasturba (1944)** died in detention.
- ✍️ **Significance:** won no *immediate* freedom, **but** made British rule **ungovernable without Indian consent** and **destroyed the middle ground** of negotiation-cum-rule. After 1942, the question was only the *terms of departure*, not *whether* the British would leave.

## PART B — THE CONSTITUTIONAL DEADLOCK (1944–46)

| Plan / talks | Who | Core idea | Fate |
|---|---|---|---|
| **C.R. Formula (1944)** ⚠️ | **Rajagopalachari** (pamphlet *The Way Out*) | Joint demand for transfer of power; **plebiscite** in Muslim-majority NW & NE; if partition, a **common centre** (defence, communications, commerce). Gandhi gave **tacit approval**. | **Jinnah rejected** — wanted *only Muslims* to vote and *no* common centre. |
| **Desai–Liaquat Pact (Jan 1945)** | Bhulabhai **Desai** ↔ **Liaquat** Ali Khan | Interim govt; **Congress–League parity**; 20% seats to other minorities. | **Unauthorised** → disowned by both. |
| **Sapru (Conciliation) Committee (1944–45)** | Liberals — **Tej Bahadur Sapru**, M.R. Jayakar | **Rejected partition**; a constitution-making body with minority safeguards. | Non-official; **failed** (Apr 1945). |
| **Wavell Plan / Simla Conference (Jun 1945)** ⚠️ | Viceroy **Wavell** | Executive Council **all-Indian** except Viceroy & C-in-C (C-in-C keeps defence); **parity between caste Hindus and Muslims**. | **Failed** — Jinnah demanded the **League alone** nominate *all* Muslims (Azad, a Muslim Congress president, could not accept). Jinnah got an effective **veto**. |
| **Wavell "Breakdown" Plan (1946)** | Wavell | British to withdraw to Muslim-majority **NW & NE**, leaving the rest to Congress. | Rejected by all. |

## PART C — THE INA & THE POST-WAR UPSURGES (1945–46)

### INA (Azad Hind Fauj) ⚠️
- **First INA (1942):** raised by **Captain Mohan Singh** from Indian **PoWs** of the Japanese; **Rash Bihari Bose**'s Indian Independence League behind it.
- **1943:** **Subhas Chandra Bose (SCB)** takes over the League & INA → **Provisional Government of Azad Hind, Singapore** (2nd parallel govt). SCB called Gandhi "Father of the Nation"; Gandhi called SCB "Prince among Patriots."
- **1944:** INA advanced with the Japanese in the **Imphal–Kohima** campaign; tricolour hoisted on Indian soil at **Moirang, Manipur (Apr 1944)**. *(Not 1945.)*

### INA Trials — Red Fort (Nov 1945) ⚠️⚠️
- First joint court-martial of **Prem Sahgal, Gurbaksh Singh Dhillon, Shah Nawaz Khan** (a **Hindu, a Sikh, a Muslim** — symbol of unity). 🎯 *"Lal Quile se aayi awaaz — Sahgal, Dhillon, Shah Nawaz!"*
- **INA Defence Committee:** Bhulabhai Desai, Tej Bahadur Sapru, **Nehru** (donned the gown after 25 yrs), K.N. Katju, Asaf Ali, Sarat Chandra Bose.
- Sentenced to transportation for life → **C-in-C Auchinleck remitted** the sentences under public pressure → showed the **Indian Army was no longer a reliable instrument of the Raj**.

### Three post-war upsurges (Bipan Chandra) 🧠
1. **21–24 Nov 1945, Calcutta** — over the **INA trials**.
2. **11–13 Feb 1946, Calcutta** — over the **7-yr sentence to INA officer Abdul Rashid** ("Rashid Ali Day").
3. **18–23 Feb 1946 — RIN Mutiny.**

### RIN Mutiny (Feb 1946) ⚠️
- Started on **HMIS Talwar**, Bombay (**B.C. Dutt** scrawled "Quit India"/"Jai Hind"). Ratings hoisted **INC + ML + CPI** flags together.
- Grievances: bad food ("**No food, no work**"), pay/pension/promotion, racial ill-treatment.
- **Naval Central Strike Committee** — **M.S. Khan & Madan Singh**. Spread to Karachi, Madras, Calcutta, Aden — 78+ ships.
- **Neither Congress nor League supported**; only **Aruna Asaf Ali** (and CPI) did. **Gandhi:** *"ill-timed and ill-advised."* **Patel** persuaded surrender (ratings surrendered "to Patel & INC, not the British").
- Called the "**final nail in the coffin**" — the armed forces, the ultimate guarantor of the Raj since 1858, were no longer certainly loyal.

## PART D — THE ENDGAME (1945–1947)

### Elections 1945–46 ⚠️
- **Central Assembly under the GoI Act, 1919; Provincial under the GoI Act, 1935.** *(Note the split — a favourite trap.)*
- **Congress**: 57/102 central seats; majority in **8/11 provinces** (incl. NWFP, Assam); ~**91% of non-Muslim** vote.
- **League**: all 30 Muslim central seats; won Sindh & Bengal; ~**87% of Muslim** vote → each party a **near-monopoly** of its community → negotiation near-impossible.

### Cabinet Mission Plan (1946) ⚠️⚠️
- Members: **Pethick-Lawrence** (SoS for India), **Stafford Cripps** (Pres., Board of Trade), **A.V. Alexander** (First Lord of the Admiralty). Arrived **24 Mar 1946**.
- **Rejected Pakistan** (minority not truly a "minority"; no strategic depth; costly division of army/railways; truncated Pakistan).
- **Three-tier scheme:**
  - **Union** — defence, foreign affairs, communications only.
  - **Groups / Sections** — **A** (Hindu-majority: UP, CP, Bihar, Madras, Bombay), **B** (NW Muslim: Punjab, NWFP, Sindh, Balochistan), **C** (NE Muslim: Bengal, Assam).
  - **Provinces** — provincial autonomy; **residuary powers with the PROVINCES**.
- 🎯 **Fatal flaw:** whether **grouping** was **compulsory** (Mission/League) or **optional** (Congress). ML accepted (6 Jun), Congress accepted (24 Jun) — with opposite readings → doomed.

### Direct Action → Interim Government (1946)
- **16 Aug 1946 — Direct Action Day** (after ML withdrew from the plan) → **Great Calcutta Killings**.
- **2 Sep 1946 — Interim Government** sworn in; **Nehru = Vice-President** of the Executive Council.
- **Muslim League joined only on 26 Oct 1946** — "to realise Pakistan" (i.e. to obstruct from within). *(Not 14 Oct.)*

### Towards freedom ⚠️
- **20 Feb 1947 — Attlee's statement:** British to **quit by June 1948**; power may be transferred to provinces in some cases.
- **21 Feb 1947 — Mountbatten** named **last Viceroy** (arrived 22 Mar 1947).
- **Balkan Plan / Dickie-Bird Plan** (Ismay): power to each province/state, free to be independent → **rejected by Nehru**.
- **Mountbatten Plan / 3 June Plan (1947):** Bengal & Punjab assemblies vote in two parts; **referendum in NWFP & Sylhet** (both chose Pakistan); Sindh to decide; two dominions with two Constituent Assemblies; **Boundary Commissions** (Radcliffe); **states may join India or Pakistan — no independence**; interim rule under **GoI Act, 1935**.

### Indian Independence Act, 1947 ⚠️⚠️
Royal Assent **18 July 1947**; effective **15 Aug 1947**. Provisions:
1. Two dominions — **India & Pakistan** — with right to secede from the Commonwealth.
2. Constituent Assemblies made **sovereign** legislatures for their dominions.
3. Office of **Secretary of State for India abolished**.
4. Title "**Emperor of India**" dropped from the Royal Style.
5. **Paramountcy lapsed** over princely states (technically free; pressed to accede).
6. Governor-General a constitutional head (advice binding); a **common GG** allowed for two dominions.
7. **Viceroy** title abolished; **GoI Act, 1935** to govern until new constitutions.
- **India:** Mountbatten first GG (then **C. Rajagopalachari**, 1948). **Pakistan:** **Jinnah** first GG (Liaquat = PM).
- Partition split: **Pakistan got ~1/3 of the army, ~17.5% of assets** → a "**moth-eaten and truncated**" Pakistan.

## 60-Second Last-Minute Revision Box ⚡

- **8 Aug 1942** Quit India Resolution, **Gowalia Tank**, moved by **Nehru** / **"Do or Die."**
- **Big-3 Prati Sarkars:** Ballia (Chittu Pandey) · Tamluk (*Tamralipta*, Vidyut Vahini) · **Satara (Prati Sarkar, Nana Patil, Toofan Sena — longest).**
- **Stayed away:** CPI · ML · Hindu Mahasabha · princely states · Ambedkar.
- **Plans order:** C.R. Formula (1944) → Desai-Liaquat (1945) → Sapru (44-45) → **Wavell/Simla (1945)** → Cabinet Mission (1946) → Attlee (Feb 1947) → **3 June Plan** → **IIA 1947**.
- **INA trio:** **Sahgal–Dhillon–Shah Nawaz** (Hindu-Sikh-Muslim); Auchinleck remitted.
- **RIN Mutiny (Feb 1946):** HMIS Talwar, B.C. Dutt; Patel ended it; Gandhi disapproved.
- **Elections:** Centre = **1919 Act**, Provinces = **1935 Act**.
- **Cabinet Mission:** Pethick-Lawrence + Cripps + Alexander; **rejected Pakistan**; 3-tier; **residuary → provinces**; killed by **grouping** dispute.
- **IIA 1947:** assent 18 Jul; two dominions; paramountcy lapses; SoS abolished; Pakistan ~1/3 army, ~17.5% assets.

## Factual corrections made while digitising (vs the source doc)
1. **INA / Imphal:** the mainland flag was hoisted at **Moirang (Apr 1944)** during the 1944 Imphal–Kohima campaign — the doc's "Imphal Campaign in 1945" is corrected to **1944**.
2. **Muslim League joined the Interim Government on 26 October 1946**, not "14 October."
3. **J.P. Narayan escaped from Hazaribagh Central Jail** (Nov 1942); the doc's "Deoli jail" is corrected.
4. **A.V. Alexander** = **First Lord of the Admiralty** (doc: "First Chief of Admiralty"); **Y.B. Chavan** (doc: "Y.V. Chavan").
5. Kept faithful to Adarsh's notes otherwise; enrichment (denial policy, "People's War" line, vote-share figures) flagged as value-addition.
