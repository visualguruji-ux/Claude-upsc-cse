Quit India Movement → Independence & Partition

The final phase of the freedom struggle, 1942–1947 · Spectrum + Bipan Chandra + Sumit Sarkar enriched · screenshot-friendly.

A Quit India — build-up & launch

Cripps Mission · Mar 1942
Stafford Cripps — post-war Dominion Status, a Constituent Assembly, provinces' right to opt out. Gandhi: "post-dated cheque on a crashing bank."
Why 1942
Cripps' failure · Japanese threat (fall of Rangoon/Singapore) · price-rise & "denial policy" · Gandhi's radicalisation.
14 Jul 1942
Wardha Resolution (CWC) — immediate British withdrawal.
8 Aug 1942 · Gowalia Tank, Bombay
Quit India Resolution (AICC) — moved by Nehru, seconded by Patel. Call: "Do or Die" (Karo ya Maro).
9 Aug 1942 · arrests
Gandhi/Kasturba/Mahadev Desai → Aga Khan Palace, Poona; CWC → Ahmednagar Fort. Aruna Asaf Ali hoists the flag.

B Course of the movement

3 phases Urban (Aug '42) → Rural, most violent (Sep–Oct '42) → Underground (late '42–44).
Underground
Usha Mehta — Congress Radio · Ram Manohar Lohia — Inquilab · Biju Patnaik — clandestine flights · J.P. Narayan — Azad Dasta, Hazaribagh escape.
Parallel governments (Big 3)
  • Ballia (U.P.) — Chittu Pandey — ~a week.
  • Tamluk (Bengal) — Tamralipta Jatiya Sarkar, Satish Samanta — arm Vidyut Vahini.
  • Satara (Maha.) — Prati Sarkar, Nana Patil — longest; Toofan Sena, nyaydan mandals.
also ContaiTalcher
Martyrs Matangini Hazra ("Gandhi Buri", Tamluk) · Kanaklata Barua (Gohpur, Assam) · Laxman Nayak (Koraput).
🧠 Stayed away: CPI (People's War) · Muslim League · Hindu Mahasabha (Savarkar: "stick to your posts") · Princely states · Ambedkar.
Suppression / significance
Linlithgow: "most serious rebellion since 1857." Gandhi's 21-day fast (Feb 1943), released 6 May 1944. → Made the Raj ungovernable without Indian consent; after 1942 only the terms of departure remained.

C Constitutional deadlock (1944–46)

C.R. Formula 1944 Rajaji's The Way Out — plebiscite in Muslim-majority NW/NE + common centre. Gandhi tacit yes; Jinnah rejected.
Desai–Liaquat Pact 1945 Interim govt, Congress–League parity + 20% minorities. Unauthorised → disowned.
Sapru Committee 44–45 Liberal, non-official; rejected partition.
Wavell Plan / Simla · Jun 1945 All-Indian Council (bar Viceroy & C-in-C) · caste-Hindu–Muslim parity. Failed — Jinnah wanted the League alone to name all Muslims.
Breakdown Plan 1946 British to pull back to Muslim NW/NE, rest to Congress. Rejected by all.

D INA & the post-war upsurges

INA / Azad Hind Fauj
1942 Capt. Mohan Singh (PoWs) + Rash Bihari Bose → 1943 Subhas Chandra Bose, Azad Hind govt (Singapore) → 1944 Moirang, Manipur flag.
INA Trials · Red Fort · Nov 1945
Sahgal · Dhillon · Shah Nawaz (Hindu-Sikh-Muslim). Defence: Desai, Sapru, Nehru… Auchinleck remitted the sentences.
3 upsurges Nov 1945 (INA trials) · Feb 1946 (Rashid Ali) · RIN Mutiny.
RIN Mutiny · Feb 1946
HMIS Talwar, B.C. Dutt · Naval Central Strike Committee (M.S. Khan). Neither Congress nor League backed it (only Aruna Asaf Ali). Patel ended it; Gandhi: "ill-timed." → "final nail in the coffin."

E The endgame (1945–47)

Elections 1945–46 Centre = GoI Act 1919; Provinces = GoI Act 1935. Congress ~91% non-Muslim vote; League ~87% Muslim vote → each a near-monopoly.
Cabinet Mission 1946
Pethick-Lawrence · Cripps · A.V. Alexander. Rejected Pakistan. 3-tier: Union · Groups (A/B/C) · Provinces; residuary → provinces. Killed by the grouping (compulsory vs optional) dispute.
  • 16 Aug 1946 Direct Action Day → Great Calcutta Killings.
  • 2 Sep 1946 Interim Govt; Nehru VP of Executive Council.
  • 26 Oct 1946 Muslim League joins Interim Govt ("to realise Pakistan").
  • 20 Feb 1947 Attlee — quit by June 1948; Mountbatten last Viceroy.
  • 3 Jun 1947 Mountbatten Plan — Bengal/Punjab split-vote; referendum NWFP & Sylhet; states join a dominion (no independence).
Indian Independence Act · assent 18 Jul 1947
  • Two dominions (15 Aug); Constituent Assemblies made sovereign.
  • Secretary of State's office abolished; "Emperor of India" title dropped.
  • Paramountcy lapsed over princely states.
  • Pakistan got ~1/3 of the army, ~17.5% of assets → "moth-eaten" Pakistan.
