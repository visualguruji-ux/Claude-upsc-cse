## 🅐 Section A — How this block is asked (pattern analysis)

| Pattern | Who loves it | What to drill |
|---|---|---|
| **Chronology / sequencing** of 1946–47 plans & events | UPSC Prelims, UPPSC, BPSC | Cabinet Mission → Direct Action → Interim Govt → Attlee → 3 June Plan → IIA |
| **Match** (person ↔ parallel govt / place; plan ↔ author) | UPSC, MPPSC, RPSC | Chittu Pandey/Samanta/Nana Patil; C.R./Wavell/Cripps |
| **Statement-based (which/how many correct)** | UPSC Prelims | Cabinet Mission 3-tier; IIA 1947 provisions; Wavell Plan |
| **"How many" membership** | UPSC (workhorse) | INA trio; who stayed away; detention venues |
| **Odd-one-out / wrong pair** | UPSC, UPPSC | phases, martyrs, upsurges |
| **Quote / "who said"** | UPSC, UPPSC | "post-dated cheque", "Do or Die", "most serious rebellion since 1857" |
| **Statute-locus traps** | UPSC Prelims | 1945–46 elections: Centre = **1919 Act**, Provinces = **1935 Act** |

**Memorise cold:** Quit India = **8 Aug 1942, Gowalia Tank, moved by Nehru** · INA trio = **Sahgal–Dhillon–Shah Nawaz** · Cabinet Mission = **Pethick-Lawrence + Cripps + Alexander**, *rejected Pakistan*, *residuary → provinces* · **IIA 1947** assent **18 Jul 1947** · League joined Interim Govt **26 Oct 1946**.

## 🅑 Section B — Practice MCQs (PYQ-pattern)

### ⏱️ Q1 — Chronology *(UPSC/UPPSC favourite)*
Arrange in correct order: 1. Cripps Mission · 2. Wavell Plan (Simla) · 3. Cabinet Mission Plan · 4. Quit India Resolution
(a) 1-4-2-3  (b) 4-1-2-3  (c) 1-4-3-2  (d) 1-2-4-3
<details><summary>Answer</summary><b>(a) 1-4-2-3</b> → Cripps (Mar 1942) → Quit India (Aug 1942) → Wavell/Simla (Jun 1945) → Cabinet Mission (1946).</details>

### 🔗 Q2 — Match parallel governments
| Parallel govt | Leader |
|---|---|
| A. Ballia | 1. Nana Patil |
| B. Tamluk (Tamralipta Jatiya Sarkar) | 2. Chittu Pandey |
| C. Satara (Prati Sarkar) | 3. Satish Chandra Samanta |
(a) A-2, B-3, C-1  (b) A-1, B-3, C-2  (c) A-2, B-1, C-3  (d) A-3, B-2, C-1
<details><summary>Answer</summary><b>(a) A-2, B-3, C-1.</b> Ballia→Chittu Pandey; Tamluk→Samanta; Satara→Nana Patil (with Y.B. Chavan; longest-surviving, Toofan Sena).</details>

### 🔢 Q3 — How many stayed away *(workhorse)*
How many of the following **did NOT support** the Quit India Movement?
1. Communist Party of India  2. Muslim League  3. Hindu Mahasabha  4. B.R. Ambedkar  5. Princely rulers
(a) Only three  (b) Only four  (c) All five  (d) Only two
<details><summary>Answer</summary><b>(c) All five.</b> CPI (People's War), ML, Hindu Mahasabha (Savarkar's "stick to your posts"), Ambedkar ("irresponsible"), princely states (muted).</details>

### 🏛️ Q4 — Cabinet Mission (statement-based) *(UPSC style)*
Consider the following about the Cabinet Mission Plan (1946):
1. It accepted a sovereign Pakistan with a truncated territory.
2. It proposed a three-tier structure of Union, groups and provinces.
3. Residuary powers were to vest in the Union centre.
How many are correct?
(a) 1 only  (b) 2 only  (c) 2 and 3  (d) 1 and 3
<details><summary>Answer</summary><b>(b) 2 only.</b> (1) wrong — it <b>rejected</b> Pakistan. (3) wrong — residuary powers were to vest in the <b>provinces</b>, not the centre.</details>

### 🪖 Q5 — INA trials membership
Who among the following was **NOT** an accused in the first Red Fort INA court-martial (1945)?
(a) Prem Sahgal  (b) Gurbaksh Singh Dhillon  (c) Shah Nawaz Khan  (d) Mohan Singh
<details><summary>Answer</summary><b>(d) Mohan Singh.</b> He raised the first INA but was not tried in the celebrated first trial (Sahgal–Dhillon–Shah Nawaz).</details>

### 🗳️ Q6 — Statute-locus trap *(high-yield)*
Regarding the 1945–46 elections, which is correct?
(a) Both central and provincial elections were held under the GoI Act, 1935
(b) Central under the GoI Act, 1919; provincial under the GoI Act, 1935
(c) Central under the GoI Act, 1935; provincial under the GoI Act, 1919
(d) Both under the GoI Act, 1919
<details><summary>Answer</summary><b>(b).</b> Central Assembly elections ran under the <b>1919</b> Act, provincial under the <b>1935</b> Act — a favourite trap.</details>

### 🧠 Q7 — "Who said it"
"The most serious rebellion since the Revolt of 1857" — this description of the Quit India Movement is attributed to:
(a) Lord Wavell  (b) Lord Linlithgow  (c) Lord Mountbatten  (d) Winston Churchill
<details><summary>Answer</summary><b>(b) Lord Linlithgow</b> (Viceroy, 1942).</details>

### ❌ Q8 — Identify the wrong pair
(a) Congress Radio — Usha Mehta
(b) Azad Dasta — Jayaprakash Narayan
(c) Clandestine flights — Ram Manohar Lohia
(d) *Inquilab* magazine — Aruna Asaf Ali & Ram Manohar Lohia
<details><summary>Answer</summary><b>(c) is wrong</b> — the clandestine flights were flown by <b>Biju Patnaik</b>, not Lohia.</details>

### 🔁 Q9 — Assertion–Reason *(UPSC A-R pattern)*
**A:** The RIN Mutiny (Feb 1946) is called a "final nail in the coffin" of the Raj.
**R:** Both the Congress and the Muslim League gave the mutiny their full official support.
(a) Both true, R explains A  (b) Both true, R not the explanation  (c) A true, R false  (d) A false, R true
<details><summary>Answer</summary><b>(c) A true, R false.</b> Neither party backed the mutiny (only Aruna Asaf Ali & the CPI); Patel got the ratings to surrender.</details>

### 📜 Q10 — IIA 1947 (how many)
How many of the following are provisions of the Indian Independence Act, 1947?
1. Abolition of the office of Secretary of State for India
2. Lapse of paramountcy over princely states
3. Dropping of the title "Emperor of India"
4. Sovereign Constituent Assemblies for the two dominions
(a) Only two  (b) Only three  (c) All four  (d) Only one
<details><summary>Answer</summary><b>(c) All four.</b> All are genuine provisions (Royal Assent 18 July 1947).</details>

### 🗓️ Q11 — Single fact *(State-PSC one-liner)*
The Muslim League joined the Interim Government on:
(a) 2 September 1946  (b) 26 October 1946  (c) 16 August 1946  (d) 20 February 1947
<details><summary>Answer</summary><b>(b) 26 October 1946</b> (the Interim Govt itself was sworn in on 2 Sep 1946).</details>

### 🤝 Q12 — Odd one out (constitutional plans)
Which plan **rejected** the demand for partition/Pakistan?
(a) C.R. Formula  (b) Cabinet Mission Plan  (c) Wavell Breakdown Plan  (d) Mountbatten Plan
<details><summary>Answer</summary><b>(b) Cabinet Mission Plan</b> — it explicitly rejected a sovereign Pakistan and proposed a united, three-tier India.</details>

## 🅒 Section C — Mains practice (GS-I, ~150–250 words)

1. *"The Quit India Movement won no immediate freedom, yet it decisively changed the terms of the Raj's continuance."* Critically examine.
2. Assess the role of **women and underground leaders** in sustaining the 1942 movement. *(Aruna Asaf Ali, Usha Mehta, Sucheta Kripalani, Matangini Hazra.)*
3. *"The INA trials and the RIN Mutiny mattered less as military events than as tests of the loyalty of the armed forces."* Discuss.
4. Why did the **Cabinet Mission Plan** fail despite acceptance by both the Congress and the League? Was partition thereafter inevitable?
5. Evaluate the **constitutional experiments of 1944–47** (C.R. Formula → Mountbatten Plan) as a search for a formula to hold India together.
