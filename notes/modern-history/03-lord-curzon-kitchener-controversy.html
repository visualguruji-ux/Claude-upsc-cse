Lord Curzon: the Kitchener Controversy & His Departure (Nov 1905)

The handwritten note's anchor - the army "dual control" quarrel that toppled a Viceroy - built into a one-stop revision sheet with Curzon's full reform record.

Spectrum, Bipan Chandra, NCERT + coaching (Vajiram / Insights / GKToday), web-verified. Every block fits the screen - screenshot any panel.

Part 1

Curzon in one glance (Viceroy 1899-1905)

Identity - most energetic & imperialist Viceroy Attitude - wanted Congress "to die peacefully" Style - efficiency + centralisation ("Commission Raj") Successor - Lord Minto II (1905-10)
He set out to deliver the Congress's funeral oration - and instead, through the Partition of Bengal, breathed militant new life into it.
Part 2 - THE ANCHOR

Kitchener-Curzon Controversy (1904-05)

The two men & the split of power

Curzon = Viceroy (G.G.)
supreme civil authority
Kitchener = C-in-C (from 1902)
army command & operations

"Dual control" of the Indian Army

C-in-C (Kitchener)
Military command & operations - the fighting side.
Military Member of Council
Army administration - supplies, transport, ordnance, finance; advises the Viceroy.
The exact phrase in the handwritten note

The Military Member was the "eyes & ears of the Governor-General in the Army" - the civilian check that kept the C-in-C answerable to the Viceroy. This is precisely the post Kitchener wanted abolished.

The clash

  • Kitchener: abolish the Military Member - concentrate all army authority (command + administration) in the C-in-C (himself).
  • Curzon: keep it - it safeguards civilian supremacy over the military.
  • Core issue: civil authority vs military authority

How it was decided & the fall

Dispute referred to London - Secy of State St John Brodrick
v
May 1905: British Cabinet backs Kitchener (PM Balfour) - Military Member abolished, replaced by a weaker "Military Supply Member"
v
Final break over the new post's powers/appointee - Curzon resigns (Aug 1905)
v
Leaves India - Nov 1905 → succeeded by Lord Minto II
Why it matters

A rare case where the soldier beat the Viceroy - showing Kitchener's prestige and Curzon's isolation. It momentarily weakened civilian control of the army (the Military Member was later restored in modified form).

Part 3 - PRELIMS GOLD

Curzon's administrative record

EducationRaleigh Commission 1902 → Indian Universities Act 1904 (tightened Govt control; Gokhale called it "retrograde").
PoliceFraser Police Commission 1902 → created the CID (provinces) & Central Intelligence.
HeritageAncient Monuments Preservation Act 1904; revived the ASI (John Marshall) - his one praised legacy.
Famine / IrrigationMacDonnell Famine Commission 1901; Scott-Moncrieff Irrigation Commission 1901.
Agriculture / EconomyImperial Institute at Pusa 1905; Cooperative Credit Societies Act 1904; Coinage & Paper Currency Act 1899 (gold standard); Railway Board 1905 (Robertson Commission).
Local GovtCalcutta Corporation Act 1899 (cut elected Indians); Punjab Land Alienation Act 1900.
Frontier / ForeignCreated NWFP 1901; Younghusband Mission to Tibet 1903-04 (the "Great Game").

🧠 Mnemonic "PACU-C": Police(CID)/Pusa - Ancient Monuments - Calcutta Corp & Coinage - Universities Act - Commerce dept + NWFP.

Part 4

Partition of Bengal (1905) - his most fateful act

  • Announced July 1905 · Effective 16 Oct 1905.
  • Official reason: administrative convenience (Bengal too large, ~78 m).
  • Real motive: "Divide et Impera" - split Bengali unity & sow a Hindu-Muslim wedge (E. Bengal & Assam = Muslim-majority).
  • Result: ignited the Swadeshi & Boycott Movement - launch pad of the Extremist phase.
  • Annulled 1911 (Delhi Durbar; capital Calcutta → Delhi).
Part 5 - PYQ & TRAPS

What the exam asks

  • UPSC Prelims (verbatim): the controversy = "Kitchener wanted abolition of the Military Member and Curzon opposed it." (Not about annulling Partition/Egypt/pacifism.)
  • One-liners: Universities Act & Ancient Monuments Act → 1904; NWFP → 1901; CID → 1902 Police Commission; Tibet → 1903-04; Partition → announced 1905, annulled 1911.
  • ⚠️ Trap: Military-Member controversy = civil vs military control, NOT the Partition - though both are 1905.
  • Mains (GS-I): "Curzon's reforms served imperial efficiency, not Indian welfare - discuss." / Partition → Swadeshi → Extremist phase.
Part 6

⏱️ 60-second revision

  • Curzon = Viceroy 1899-1905; wanted Congress to die.
  • Kitchener (C-in-C, 1902) wanted the Military Member ("eyes & ears of the Viceroy in the Army") abolished → all army control to himself.
  • London (Brodrick / PM Balfour) backed Kitchener → post replaced by weak Military Supply Member.
  • Curzon resigned Aug 1905 → left India Nov 1905 → Lord Minto II.
  • Key Acts: Universities & Ancient Monuments 1904, Calcutta Corp & Coinage 1899, NWFP 1901, CID (1902), Pusa & Railway Board 1905.
  • Partition of Bengal → 16 Oct 1905 → Swadeshi; annulled 1911.
