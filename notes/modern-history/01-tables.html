Table 1 - Pre-Congress Associations: Phase 1 (1830s-1850s)

Landed / aristocratic elites, provincial in character

YearAssociationCityFounder / Key figures and points
1836Bangabhasha Prakashika SabhaCalcuttaAssociates of Raja Rammohan Roy. Among the earliest political associations.
1838Landholders' Society (Zamindari Association)CalcuttaDwarkanath Tagore, Raja Radhakant Deb. First organised political effort; constitutional agitation; safeguarded zamindar (landlord) interests.
1843Bengal British India SocietyCalcuttaOn the advice of George Thompson. Aim: collect and publish info on the real condition of the people.
1851British Indian AssociationCalcuttaMerger of Landholders' Society + Bengal British India Society (Pres.: Raja Radhakant Deb; Secy.: Debendranath Tagore). 1852 petition to Parliament: separate legislature, separation of executive and judiciary, abolition of certain taxes.
1852Bombay AssociationBombayJagannath Shankar Seth, Dadabhai Naoroji. First political association of the Bombay Presidency; petitions on the Charter Act 1853.
1852Madras Native AssociationMadrasGazulu Lakshminarasu Chetty. Raised ryotwari peasant grievances; short-lived.
1852Deccan AssociationPoona regionLocal reformers. Short-lived; tried to send a mission to England for Charter Act 1853 reforms, but failed.

Table 2 - Pre-Congress Associations: Phase 2 (1860s-1880s)

More nationalist, broader-based

YearAssociationFounders and points to remember
1865London Indian SocietyDadabhai Naoroji, W.C. Bonnerjee (London). Soon merged into the East India Association.
1866East India AssociationDadabhai Naoroji (London). Build British public opinion / influence MPs in favour of India; later had branches in India.
1870Poona Sarvajanik SabhaG.V. Joshi, S.H. Sathe, S. Chiplunkar (Ranade joined 1871; not a founder). 1875 petition to the House of Commons for direct representation, signed by 23,000+; nurtured Gokhale and Tilak.
1876Indian National AssociationSurendranath Banerjee, Anand Mohan Bose (Calcutta). First avowedly nationalist body; led the Ilbert Bill and Civil Service age-limit agitations; merged into the INC in 1886.
1878National Mohammedan AssociationSyed Amir Ali. A political platform for Muslim concerns.
1884Madras Mahajan SabhaG. Subramania Iyer, P. Anandacharlu, M. Viraraghavachariar. Forerunner of the Congress in the South.
1885Bombay Presidency AssociationPherozeshah Mehta, Badruddin Tyabji, K.T. Telang - the "Bombay Trio". Demanded a larger Indian role in councils and services.
1893Indian Parliamentary CommitteeWilliam Wedderburn, W.S. Caine (Britain). Gather support for Indian reform among the British public.
