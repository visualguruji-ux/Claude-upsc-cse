## 2. Exam Structure (quick reference)

**Stage 1 — Prelims (qualifying, objective, MCQ):**
- **GS Paper I** (100 Q, 200 marks) — merit-deciding. Negative marking 1/3.
- **CSAT Paper II** (80 Q, 200 marks) — qualifying only, need 33% (66/200).

**Stage 2 — Mains (written, descriptive, 1750 marks + 275 interview = 2025 total):**
- Paper A: Compulsory Indian Language (qualifying, 300)
- Paper B: English (qualifying, 300)
- **Essay** (250)
- **GS-I** — Heritage, History, Geography, Society (250)
- **GS-II** — Polity, Governance, Constitution, IR (250)
- **GS-III** — Economy, Environment, Sci-Tech, Security, DM (250)
- **GS-IV** — Ethics, Integrity, Aptitude (250)
- **Optional Paper I & II — Sociology** (250 + 250 = 500)

**Stage 3 — Personality Test / Interview (275)**

## 3. Sociology Optional — Strategy Notes

- **Why it fits Adarsh:** short syllabus, high overlap with GS-I (Indian Society),
  Essay, and Ethics; scoring with good answer structure; no prior background needed.
- **Paper I:** Fundamentals of Sociology (thinkers — Marx, Weber, Durkheim, Parsons,
  Merton, Mead; perspectives; research methods; social structures & institutions).
- **Paper II:** Indian Society (Indological/structural-functional/Marxist views;
  caste, class, kinship, agrarian & industrial change, secularism, social movements).
- **Core sources (standard):** Haralambos & Holborn (Themes/Perspectives),
  Ritzer (thinkers), NCERT Sociology (XI–XII), Nitin Sangwan / IGNOU notes for Indian
  society, Ram Ahuja for Indian Social Problems.
- **Answer edge:** cite thinkers + Indian examples + current sociological data
  (NFHS, Census, NSSO, NCRB) + diagrams where useful.
